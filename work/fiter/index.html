Fiter

Infrastructure first. Design second.
Fiter

Press in Forbes and Folha. Invisible in search.

Fiter is a Brazilian HR and EdTech SaaS that uses AI to predict turnover, measure climate and reduce student dropout. It had coverage in Folha de SP, Estadão, Forbes Brasil and CBN, and none of it showed up in search. Nine critical issues were logged before any design decision.

Client
Fiter · via Vellumwire
Timeline
2025 · 2 weeks
Role
Strategy, IA, copy, design system
Team
Afonso, front-end and hosting
01

The situation

A visual presence with nothing behind it.

One Joomla page for two products. The title tag read “fiter”, the meta description read “parallax one page”, the theme’s name. GTM was installed but never configured, and the blog lived on a subdomain.

  • 22/100SEO score
  • 1page for two products
  • 0conversion events tracked
  • 4press outlets, unused
02

Decision

Two buyers, two paths.

An HR director and a university dean search for different things. One page can’t rank for both, so each ecosystem got its own pages, intent and CTA inside the same domain.

03

Architecture

Sixteen pages, three clusters.

Two separate domains were rejected: they would have split the press backlinks in half. The blog came back from the subdomain to fiter.com.br/blog.

04

SEO

Intent matched to page type.

Titles, metas, one H1 per page and schema on all 16 pages, mapped to five keyword clusters across the funnel.

05

Outcome

From 22 to 100, in every category.

Every category rebuilt from near zero, inside the same two weeks, with GA4 and Search Console live from launch day.

Results

A site that counts what it earns.

  • 22 → 100SEO score, all 7 categories
  • 1 → 16pages fully structured
  • 0 → 16pages with schema markup
  • 3leads attributed in the first weeks, from zero
