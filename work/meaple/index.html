Meaple

Five apps for one Friday night
Meaple

One product for the whole night out.

Ensight set out to own the full journey of a night out in Brazil, before, during and after, in one product: discovery, social coordination, ticket sales and payment inside the event. I joined with a blank slate, no brief, no wireframes, no research, and defined what the product should be before anyone touched a screen.

Role
Design Lead
Team
Solo design, 2 engineers, 2 co-founders
Client
Ensight · 2020
Platform
iOS 10+ · Android 4.4+, then web
01

The problem

Five apps for one Friday night.

Sympla sold tickets. WhatsApp moved logistics. Facebook had the social graph but had abandoned events. Instagram had discovery with no way to act. PicPay moved money with no idea what it was for.

02

Research

Different people, the same fragmentation.

I interviewed users between 19 and 30, event creators and venue owners. Nobody asked for one app, because nobody imagined it possible. That third column became the product thesis.

03

Business

Revenue from day one, or no product.

Before the pandemic, events moved around R$250 billion a year in Brazil, about 4.3% of GDP (Abrafesta; Abeoc/Sebrae). Ensight set the targets before design started, and they ruled out a slow-burn social network.

  • 150kmonthly active users
  • 1.5Mdownloads
  • R$2.00CAC ceiling per user
  • R$2.70revenue per user per month
04

Decision

Producers first.

My first instinct was consumers first. That is wrong for a marketplace: one producer brings their whole audience, one consumer brings only themselves. Producers got ticket sales, analytics and a page before the audience existed.

05

Decision

Three accounts, hard boundaries.

Friends, individual professionals and companies need different permissions. A two-tier model confused them, so it went.

06

Decision

Momentum: discovery that ranks trust.

Momentum ranked the Explore feed on five inputs. Producer credibility weighed most: a new account ranked below a producer with ten well-rated events.

07

Decision

The comanda came later.

Ordering and paying at the bar inside the app was not in the initial scope. It came in later in the MVP: consumption that producers could capture instead of losing it to cash and card machines.

08

Design

Designed for the worst moment.

A loud venue, one hand, a drink, interruptions. Every rule of the interface came from that user.

09

Design system

25 screens, one system.

Discovery, the social graph, transactions, the comanda and the producer side, designed end to end with the brand.

Outcome

The pandemic closed the market. Meaple pivoted to web.

Meaple launched as an app. When COVID-19 made gatherings illegal, it pivoted to a website to start earning, with the plan to return as an app. It is live at meaple.com.br.

  • 350kevents canceled in Brazil in 2020
  • 98%of the events sector hit
  • R$230Blost across 2020 and 2021

Sources: Sebrae; Abrape; Abrafesta via G1; Abeoc/Sebrae.
