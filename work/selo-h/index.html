Selo H

The product had credibility. The site didn’t.
Selo H

A certification as serious as its website.

Selo H is Brazil’s first workplace mental health certification built to international standards: NR-01, Lei 14.831/24, WHO guidelines and ICD-11. Alelo, Amil, Arcor, Claro and Cogna were already certified. When the old domain lapsed, the site had to be rebuilt from scratch in one week.

Client
Selo H · via Vellumwire
Timeline
2025 · 1 week
Role
Strategy, UX/IA, copy, visual design
Team
Afonso, front-end and hosting
01

The situation

No site, no leads, no certifications.

The certification process itself runs through the website. Without it there was no channel for leads and no way to document new certified companies. The brief: make the site as serious as the certification.

02

Diagnosis

Six problems feeding each other.

Before any copy or layout, I audited what was there. One question set every decision that followed.

03

Decision

Outcome first, proof where it decides.

The hero leads with what the buyer wants, legal anchors sit right under it, each step explains why it exists, and certified companies close the page right before the form.

04

Decision

One action, from hero to footer.

Newsletter, downloads and alternate contacts gave undecided visitors a way out. Now “Solicitar certificação” is the only ask on the page, repeated at every scroll position.

05

Build

A certification that loads like one.

A slow site contradicts a product sold on rigor. The rebuild dropped the template for clean HTML and CSS.

Results

Measured in the first 30 days. No ads, no outreach.

  • 5×more leads than the pre-launch baseline
  • +50%conversion rate
  • −40%bounce rate
  • 2.3×time on page
  • 1.4 sLCP, down from 4.8 s
  • 100Lighthouse score
