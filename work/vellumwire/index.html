Vellumwire: A studio site that closes before the call

A studio site that closes before the call
Vellumwire

The studio was its own first client.

Vellumwire is the web design studio I run with Afonso, for service businesses that need more inbound leads. There was no site, no positioning and no portfolio yet. The site had to earn trust from people burned by agencies before, and do the selling on its own.

Type
Own studio
Timeline
2025 · 4 weeks
Role
Strategy, copy, IA, design system
Team
Afonso, front-end and hosting
01

Diagnosis

Every competitor led with portfolio.

Nobody named the problem the prospect already had, asks were high-commitment, and nobody published prices. The same six objections came up in every sales conversation, and no site answered them.

02

Decision

The page sells like a good salesperson.

Each section answers the next question a prospect would ask on a call. By the final CTA, the problem is confirmed, the price is known and the objections are answered.

03

Decision

A 15-minute ask, not a quote.

The main CTA is a free 15-minute diagnosis on a live screen-share. The prospect keeps the fix list whether they hire the studio or not, and the session shows expertise before any proposal.

04

Decision

Prices on the page.

Publishing prices filters budget mismatches before the first call and reads as confidence to a buyer who has been burned before.

Results

Found by search, closed by the page.

A cold prospect can go from a search to a booked diagnosis without a single touchpoint from the studio.

  • ~1korganic sessions per week
  • $0ad spend, no social presence at launch
  • 6sales objections answered in writing
  • 4 weeksfrom blank file to an indexed site
