Veriflow

Pricing and plans concept for a SaaS product

UI/UX Design · 2024 · Concept project

Overview

Veriflow is a concept for a B2B pricing experience. The page explores how a complex product can compare tiers clearly, explain value without clutter, and move buyers toward the right decision.

Plan architecture: risk based SaaS pricing

The plan structure is organized around decision-making rather than feature dumping. Clear labels and a recommended tier help users compare options faster.

Value blocks: quantify fraud, fines and downtime

Supporting blocks turn product value into concrete evidence. The point is to make the pricing story feel grounded, not abstract.

Social proof: de-risk the final decision

Trust markers and outcome-focused quotes help lower friction near the end of the page. The section is meant to support the decision, not dominate it.

FAQ and footer: remove remaining objections

A structured FAQ handles the final objections before sign-up. The footer closes the page with utility links and a clear end state.

The outcome

The layout is designed to make the pricing decision feel clear and low-friction. Hierarchy does most of the persuasive work, while the content stays focused on usefulness rather than hype.
