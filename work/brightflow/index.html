BrightFlow

Customer feedback intelligence concept for a product design portfolio

UI/UX Design · Brand · 2024 · Concept project

Overview

BrightFlow is a conceptual SaaS platform for organizing customer feedback into product signals. The page explores how a complex product can present value clearly through hierarchy, modular sections, and a consistent visual system.

Flow overview & component grid

The page presents the product in three steps: capture feedback, surface patterns, and turn them into next actions. The feature section uses repeated cards to show how reusable patterns make a product easier to scan — staying compact and coherent at every breakpoint.

Dashboard surface

The dashboard focuses on hierarchy, contrast, and legibility so key signals stay easy to read. It is designed to feel practical first, with supporting data available without overwhelming the main view.

Ecosystem, trust & pricing

Trust markers and integrations frame the product as something that fits into existing workflows. The pricing section keeps the decision simple with a clear tier structure and strong visual hierarchy.

Closing the page

The final CTA and footer close with a straightforward path forward. The layout is designed to feel complete without repetition.
