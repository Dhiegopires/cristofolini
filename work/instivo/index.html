Governing what no spreadsheet can hold
Instivo

Operations that run themselves, configured by the people who own them.

Instivo is a SaaS for multi-unit retail, a company of around 50 people. I turned its business rules into a data workspace, a rule engine and a card-linked entity model that replaced six disconnected tools, and rebuilt the design system underneath.

Role
Product Designer (contract)
Period
Jun – Dec 2023
Team
Cross-functional, 5–10 per task
Research
Interviews with retail managers, usability tests
01

The problem

Six tools, and nobody in charge.

Employee records lived in spreadsheets, supplier orders went by phone and stock was counted by hand. A product dropping below 50 units triggered nothing: someone had to notice, call and follow up.

02

The product

One workspace every screen reads from.

The Data Workspace is where data is created and structured. Every form field, category and role across Instivo traces back to it. System workspaces feed the platform and cannot be deleted; free workspaces belong to the user.

03

Research

Three roles, rules that collide invisibly.

I led discovery and interviews with retail managers alongside the PM. An IT rule could restrict a manager; a manager rule could block an operator IT had allowed. Nobody saw the wall coming.

04

Decision

Cards, not folders.

The brief asked for folders. But one product belongs to several categories at once, and a folder holds it in one place. Linked cards keep one record that plays a different role in each context.

05

Decision

Rules managers build without writing logic.

A fixed four-part schema, picked from constrained chips: operator, entity, condition, action. Free text came first and failed in testing.

06

Decision

Explain the limit, don’t hide it.

Critical workspaces show the delete control disabled, with the reason in a tooltip. Inherited rules appear read-only with their origin.

07

Fleet & Dock

One record, every module.

Booking a dock needs a driver, a vehicle, a bay and a warehouse, all built in the Data Workspace. Change a driver’s license there and every booking updates. No duplication, no integration layer.

08

Design system

Four layers. A new client touches one.

The inherited system had no tokens and no responsive logic. When mobile came mid-project, the semantic layer already resolved to it.

Results

Faster to configure. Faster to open a unit.

Measured in usability testing with real users. Managers changed rules, categories and thresholds themselves, without filing IT requests.

  • ~70%less configuration time
  • 5×faster new-unit onboarding
  • 6 → 1disconnected tools replaced by one workspace
