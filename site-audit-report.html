01

Audit Findings

Six categories, scored 0-10. Every finding below references a real file, line, or visible element on the current site. Nothing generalized.

First Impression 7 / 10

Finding

Badge copy contradicts the visual-note copy six lines later. The hero badge reads Available for new projects (index.html:938), but the portrait overlay right next to it reads Open to full-time product roles (index.html:973-975). One sounds like freelance availability, the other like a job search. A recruiter skimming for 5 seconds gets a mixed signal at the exact moment the page is supposed to be unambiguous.

Fix: make both say the same thing. Given the stated goal is interviews, not freelance work, both should read closer to "Open to full-time roles."

Finding

H1 ("Design that converts.") carries no role keyword. The headline is strong and on-brand, but a recruiter who screenshots or searches the page won't find "Product Designer" anywhere in the primary heading. It only appears in the title tag and meta description.

Fix: not urgent. The role is reinforced immediately below in the lead paragraph and tags, so this is a minor SEO/skim-reinforcement gap, not a comprehension problem.

What's already working

Primary CTA ("See selected work") is visible without scrolling. Visual hierarchy is clear: badge, headline, one-line proof, two CTAs, four credibility tags. As of today, the Work section also now sits second on the page, directly after the trust-signal logo marquee, so a recruiter reaches case studies in one scroll instead of three.

Technical SEO 6 / 10

Finding

Person schema's ItemList only lists 4 of 9 case studies. index.html:61-93 hard-codes NorthPay, Veriflow, BrightFlow, and Collabboard as the "Featured product design case studies" structured data. MedMe, Instivo, Vellumwire, Vellumwire Design System, and Fiter (five live case studies) are invisible to anything reading that schema (rich results, AI crawlers summarizing the site).

Fix: regenerate the list with all 9 positions.

Finding

robots.txt blocks every PDF on the domain (Disallow: /*.pdf$). There's currently no resume file on the site at all, but if one gets added later as a plain PDF, it will be unreachable by search by default. Only relevant in combination with the missing-resume finding below.

Fix: no action needed until a resume PDF exists; flag it then.

What's already working

Title (45 chars) and meta description (~150 chars) are both within limits and carry the role and specialty. Canonical, hreflang (en / pt-BR / x-default), and sitemap.xml are all present and correctly wired across every page checked this session. Person schema itself (name, jobTitle, sameAs, knowsAbout, address) is solid. It's just missing Behance in sameAs alongside LinkedIn.

Performance 7 / 10

Finding (fixed today, not yet live)

Image payload was 50.1 MB across 179 files. Converted to WebP and resized this session: now 6.4 MB, an 87% reduction. None of this is deployed yet. It only exists in the local working tree.

Fix: commit and deploy. Until then, production is still serving the 50 MB version.

Finding (fixed today)

The hero's LCP image preload pointed at a deleted file. index.html:100 (and the pt-BR equivalent) preloaded assets/img/Photo.jpg, which no longer exists after today's WebP migration. The real image is Photo.webp. The preload hint was doing nothing, and the actual largest-contentful-paint image had no preload at all. Corrected during this audit.

Open question

Mobile rendering was not tested this session. Every check this session ran at 1440px desktop. Cannot score what hasn't been observed.

Content & Copy 8 / 10

What's already working

Every case study carries real, specific numbers (SEO 22→100, 87% adoption, 35% projected conversion lift, etc.), tied to named methods rather than vague claims. Em dashes, "not X but Y" contrast framing, and repeated AI-slop phrasing were already audited and removed earlier this session, across both languages.

Fixed today

Authorship was ambiguous in two cases. The Vellumwire case used "we" nine times despite being listed as a solo project (work/vellumwire/index.html); Vellumwire Design System used zero first-person language anywhere, so no decision was ever attributed to anyone. Both rewritten today: "we" → "I" where it was actually solo work, and "I" inserted at five real decision points in the Design System case (the layer split, the rejected client-data demo, the grid-density change, the Instivo refinement).

UX & Conversion 5 / 10

Finding (critical, fixed today, not yet verified live)

The contact form was not delivering messages. contact/contact.php called the Resend API using an environment variable (RESEND_API_KEY) that was never configured on the server, and sent from an unverified sandbox address (onboarding@resend.dev) flagged with its own unfinished // TODO comment in the code. Every real submission was either silently failing server-side or being filtered as spam. Rewritten today to use the host's native mail() function. No third-party account required. A failed-send fallback was also added: if it ever fails again, the visitor now sees a direct, clickable email address instead of a dead-end "try again."

Fix: deploy, then send one real test submission to confirm delivery before relying on it.

Finding

The stated conversion goal is "book a call," but the only contact path is an asynchronous form. There is no scheduling link (Calendly, Cal.com, or similar) anywhere on the site. A recruiter willing to act immediately is asked to write a message and wait, which is more friction than the goal requires.

Fix: add a booking link to the contact page and/or hero CTA.

Finding

No downloadable resume anywhere on the site. A recruiter who wants to forward a candidate internally or load them into an ATS typically needs a PDF, not a set of pages to summarize manually. Confirmed absent in index.html, contact/index.html, and the nav/footer across all pages checked.

Fix: add a one-click resume download in the nav or contact page.

Visual Identity 7 / 10

What's already working

Dark theme, bold/blocked typography (Bebas Neue display, Syne body), and a single gold accent (#f3b02e) are applied consistently across every page and language checked this session. The look matches the stated preference: blocked, minimalist, bold, and explicitly not light mode.

Self-identified gap

Site has less interaction and motion polish than the portfolios it's being measured against. Direct quote from this session's discovery answers: the site was built fast after losing the previous one, and competing senior portfolios are seen as having "mais interações, animações, mais cuidado." This is the gap between a 7 and a 9 here: not a structural problem, a craft-time one.

Finding

Fiter's logo is missing from the home page trust-signal marquee (index.html:1021-1039), even though Fiter is a full, live case study. Every other case-study client that has a logo asset is represented there.

Fix: add the Fiter logo to the marquee.
