02

Intent Mapping

The five highest-priority pages: who lands here, what mindset they're in, and what the page must contain to hold them.

/High
Search intent
Commercial. The visitor already has a candidate (this one) in mind, arriving from LinkedIn or a direct name search, not browsing cold.
Persona, mindset
Recruiter or hiring manager, 30 to 60 seconds budgeted, checking for role fit and seniority before deciding whether to read further.
Funnel stage
Top
Sections required
  • Unambiguous role and availability statement (badge and visual note must agree)
  • Primary proof point with real numbers, not adjectives
  • Trust signal logos
  • Direct path to case studies, not buried below the fold
Linking
Sends to: /work/, /contact/. Receives from: every external mention of the candidate's name (LinkedIn, Behance).
/work/High
Search intent
Commercial. Visitor is past the first impression and is now actively evaluating range and depth.
Persona, mindset
Same visitor as Home, second click. Now scanning for breadth (SaaS and Fintech both represented) and filtering by what matters to their specific role.
Funnel stage
Middle
Sections required
  • Filter by category (already present: All, UI/UX, Branding, Design System, 2D)
  • One-line outcome per card, not a generic description
  • Consistent card imagery that doesn't require cropping guesswork (the object-fit issue fixed earlier this session)
Linking
Sends to: all 9 case studies. Receives from: Home, every case study's "All work" link, footer.
/work/vellumwire-ds/High
Search intent
Commercial, specific. Visitor is sourcing for design-system depth specifically, the single most scope-defining skill separating senior from staff per the market research.
Persona, mindset
Hiring manager who already passed the 30-second screen and is now checking whether the candidate can reason about systems, not just screens.
Funnel stage
Bottom
Sections required
  • Explicit first-person decision points (fixed this session, five of them)
  • The four-layer architecture diagram, already present and now typo-corrected
  • A real production link-out (Instivo) proving the system shipped, not just a concept
Linking
Sends to: /work/instivo/ (explicit "read the case" link), /contact/. Receives from: /work/, Home carousel, case-nav from Vellumwire and Fiter.
/work/instivo/High
Search intent
Commercial, specific. Visitor wants proof the design system claim on the DS case page is real, not theoretical.
Persona, mindset
Hiring manager cross-referencing two pages to verify a claim, the highest-intent behavior on the entire site.
Funnel stage
Bottom
Sections required
  • The real product screen now in the hero (fixed this session, replacing a cropped card)
  • Governance and rule-engine specifics tied to real numbers (0 IT tickets, 5x onboarding speed)
Linking
Sends to: /contact/. Receives from: /work/vellumwire-ds/ direct link, /work/, Home carousel, case-nav.
/contact/High
Search intent
Transactional. Visitor has decided to act.
Persona, mindset
Ready to schedule or write, low patience for friction. This is the page where the entire site's stated goal, generating an interview, either closes or leaks.
Funnel stage
Bottom
Sections required
  • Booking link above the form, not buried (added this session)
  • Working form as a fallback for visitors who prefer async (mail() fix, pending live verification)
  • Direct email visible as a second fallback (added this session)
Linking
Receives from: every page's nav, every case study's hero secondary CTA, the site-wide footer.
