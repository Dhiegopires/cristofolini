Banned Language Check

Searched every HTML page, case-sensitive and case-insensitive, for the full banned list from the tone rules: crucial, leverage, holistic, seamless, cutting-edge, innovative, synergy, ecosystem, empower, robust, scalable solution, game-changer, and the throat-clearing openers Discover, Unlock, Transform your, Revolutionize, Welcome to.

PASS

Zero hard violations. Every hit on the banned list resolved to a legitimate, specific usage on inspection, not generic filler. "Ecosystem" appears six times, always describing a real structural split (Fiter's RH versus Educação product lines, Meaple's QuaQ and Facto sub-products), never as vague "join our ecosystem" language. "Discover" appears once, mid-sentence in the MedMe case describing a real user-flow failure ("you find a time and discover the slot you wanted is gone"), not as a headline opener. "Innovative" appears once, inside this project's own Step 3 visual-direction document, specifically listed as a word to avoid, not used as site copy.

Structural Notes

PASS

H1/H2 lock respected. No H1 or H2 was changed during this copy audit. All 12 pages still match the structure documented and approved in Step 4.1.

NOTE

The Vellumwire case's "FAQ from real objections" section has 4 entries, not the methodology's usual 6-question minimum. They are also framed as objection labels ("Budget mismatches," "Trust deficit") paired with design rationale, not literally phrased as a visitor would ask them ("How much does this cost?"). This is intentional: the section documents which real sales objections the pricing page design addresses, it is not a customer-facing FAQ widget. Inventing two more entries to hit a round number would violate the no-fabrication rule with no real data behind them. Recommendation: leave as 4, the count is a side effect of how many real objections exist, not a gap.

PASS
