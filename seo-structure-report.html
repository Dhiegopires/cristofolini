H1 / Keyword Tension Global Settings Per-Page Structure Character Validation
Flagged once, applies to all 12 pages Per the approved sitemap (Step 2.1), every page has a primary keyword. None of the 12 live H1s contain their assigned keyword literally. This is a deliberate tension, not an oversight: the H1s are narrative, voice-driven headlines ("Infrastructure first. Design second.", "Every new client started the same way: rebuilding what already existed.") that were specifically audited for AI-slop and authorship voice earlier in this project. Forcing a keyword into them ("Senior Product Designer Portfolio: Design That Converts") would read as keyword-stuffed and undo that work. Recommendation: keep H1s as-is, carry keyword signal in title tag, meta description, and H2s instead, which is what the data below shows already happening in most cases. This is a recommendation, not yet an approved decision.
